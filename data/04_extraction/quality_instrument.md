# Quality assessment instrument

Each included study is scored on four items: Yes = +1, Partially = 0, No = -1 (total from -4 to +4).

| Item | Question |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Are the study objectives clearly stated? |
| Q2 | Is there a complete description of the population and of the way the sample was selected? |
| Q3 | Does the study provide detailed information about the defect analysis, including methodology and metrics? |
| Q4 | Are the ML models clearly described, including their architecture and purpose? |

`quality_assessment.csv` lists the eligible studies of the database search with the original global score.
The score describes the evidence base and was not used to exclude studies.
